Disciplinary & Behaviour Policy

Conduct, reporting, disciplinary procedures and appeals.

← Back to Policies

Introduction and scope

Bude Hockey Club expects high standards of behaviour on and off the pitch. This policy applies to all members, coaches, managers, volunteers, parents, spectators, umpires and officials, including matters outside league disciplinary processes.

Club standards

Members must follow the club Code of Ethics and Behaviour and the Disciplinary Policy. Matters should be resolved locally and informally where appropriate, involving captains, coaches and parents or carers as necessary. England Hockey and West Hockey League regulations also apply.

Cards and misconduct

Repeated yellow cards require advice and coaching, with league accumulation rules applying. Red cards follow the West League disciplinary procedure and are reviewed by the club committee where necessary. Dissent and verbal abuse are not tolerated and must be referred by the team captain on the day. Violent conduct or threats of violence must be referred to both league and club disciplinary procedures.

Reporting an incident

Alleged breaches should be reported in writing using the original policy's Disrepute Incident Reporting Form, ideally within two days and no later than seven days after the incident. Team captains lead referrals and must self-refer if involved. The club's Disciplinary Officer receives reports.

Under-18s

Any matter involving a young person must involve the Club Welfare Officer and follow the club safeguarding procedures, including England Hockey referrals where required.

Disciplinary Committee and sanctions

The committee comprises the Club Chair, Club Secretary and relevant Ladies' or Men's Captain, and should meet within ten days of an incident requiring a decision. Possible sanctions include a written warning, voluntary contribution to the club, fine, suspension for up to six months without membership refund, or withdrawal of membership without refund. The Executive Committee must ratify sanctions. Decisions are communicated in writing within ten days of the hearing.

Appeals

A club disciplinary decision may be appealed in writing within five days of receipt. The appeal is considered by the Club Chair and two independent club officers, who should meet within ten days of receipt. The final appeal decision is communicated within three days.

Original policy and reporting form

The club's supplied six-page Disciplinary Policy contains the complete procedures and a two-page Code of Ethics & Behaviour Disrepute Incident Form. This page is a summary, not a replacement for the signed or adopted original. Contact budehockey@gmail.com for the original document or form.
