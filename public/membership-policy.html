Membership Fee Policy 2026/27

Membership categories, fees, payment arrangements and associated benefits.

← Back to Policies

Membership and registration

All individuals who attend training or play hockey for Bude Hockey Club must register as members and pay the appropriate membership fee. Membership runs from 1 October to 30 September each year. Members must pay in full or agree instalments with the Club Treasurer by 30 September. The first instalment is due by 30 September.

Members paying in full by 30 September receive a £10 reduction. Fees can also be paid in two instalments, due by 30 September and 1 January, or in six instalments. Payment is by bank transfer or standing order; cash is not accepted.

Eligibility and outstanding fees

Members who have not paid or agreed a payment plan by 30 September are ineligible to train or be selected for matches from 1 October. Outstanding membership or match fees from previous seasons must be cleared before training or selection. Transferring players must be fully paid up; annual membership fees are not refundable on transfer.

Joining later, injury and refunds

Members joining in October pay the full annual fee. Later joiners pay a pro-rata rate for the remainder of the playing season. In cases of serious injury or illness preventing participation for the rest of the season, the Club may consider a pro-rata refund on written application to the Treasurer.

What membership covers

Membership fees contribute to coaching, training, pitch hire, league matches, home fixture teas, travel costs, club affiliation and England Hockey insurance. Tournament fees are charged separately. Occasional members must register before participating and must become full members if they exceed six games.

Membership categories

Adult playing members; student or junior members playing adult hockey; junior members; occasional members; associate volunteers; honorary life members; and training-only members aged over 18. Junior ages are assessed at 1 September at the start of the season. Parents or guardians must register members under 18 and provide emergency and relevant medical information.

Insurance and review

The Club subscribes to England Hockey Membership+ insurance including public liability, abuse cover and personal accident cover, but this is not a personal injury insurance arrangement. Membership fees and categories are reviewed annually and approved at an AGM, General Meeting or by the Executive Committee if meetings are inquorate.

For financial difficulties, payment plans or membership enquiries contact budehockey@gmail.com.
