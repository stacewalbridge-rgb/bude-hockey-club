Connect club social media to the website

This page is for the authorised administrator of Bude Hockey Club's Facebook and Instagram accounts.

Connection status: setup required. The website is not yet authorised to read either account. This page does not collect passwords or access tokens.

What the social-media administrator needs to do

  1. Sign in to Meta Business Suite using your own Facebook account.
  2. Confirm that you have full access to the club's official Facebook Page (if one exists), and identify the Page name and URL. The club's Facebook Group is separate and cannot be treated as a Page.
  3. In Meta Business Suite, check that Instagram @bude.hockey is a Professional account and connected to the club's Facebook Page, where applicable.
  4. Tell Stacey whether you control the Page, Instagram account and Facebook Group. No login details or passwords should be shared.
  5. Confirm that posts and photos intended for the public website have appropriate publication permission, particularly photographs of junior players.

What happens next?

The website developer must register and configure the Meta app, redirect address, requested permissions and secure token storage. Only after that will an official Meta authorisation button be enabled here. The administrator will then approve access directly through Meta.

Facebook Group posts are not available through the same Page integration. We will separately assess a permitted way to include Group content.
