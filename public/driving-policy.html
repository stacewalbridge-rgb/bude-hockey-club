Repayment for Driving Policy

Travel-cost contribution for qualifying official vehicles travelling to league fixtures and tournaments.

Club driving policyDrivers are repaid from membership-fee income. The policy is designed as a contribution towards costs rather than full reimbursement.

Introduction

As a benefit of membership, the Club does not require players to make an “on the day” contribution towards their travel costs to a league fixture or tournament venue. The Club repays drivers using income from players’ membership fees.

The policy applies the principle that players are transported to a venue in vehicles that make as efficient use of each vehicle’s capacity as possible — in other words, as few vehicles as possible are to be used to transport players to the venue.

Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to ensure that drivers transporting players to a senior fixture or tournament receive appropriate repayment for doing so. It is not intended that the repayment covers all costs; rather, it is a contribution towards the driver’s full costs.

It excludes repayment for travel to senior tour venues. Costs for these events have to be factored into the overall cost of the event paid by each player.

Who is affected by this policy?

Anyone who transports players to matches and meets the qualifying requirements in accordance with this policy. This includes hockey venues for senior fixtures and tournaments and Club officials travelling to support junior fixtures or tournaments.

Requirements

The team captain should always confirm the match venue with the respective League website. A repayment is available to anyone who uses their vehicle to transport players to a league fixture or tournament venue and meets the following qualifying requirements:

  • Repayment is made for a vehicle transporting players from and back to Budehaven School in accordance with the repayment figures set out in the Annex.
  • Team officials — a coach and/or umpire — can be counted as “players” for the purpose of the calculation for official vehicles.
  • An official vehicle is one that has been confirmed by the captain will receive repayment.
  • Assuming a vehicle has a minimum capacity of 5 seats, including the seat occupied by the driver who is a playing member of the team, a team will require a minimum of 3 official vehicles to transport its players to and back from a match venue.
  • The Club will pay for a maximum of 4 official vehicles to transport the players to and back from a match venue.
  • Vehicles that have less than 5 seats available for players should be avoided.
  • The team captain must confirm the official vehicles to the respective drivers before the journey commences.
  • If a driver wishes to drive to a game and their vehicle is not identified as one of the official vehicles, no repayment will be made.
  • When a team includes a significant number of players who do not drive, a non-playing driver can receive repayment for driving, but only if the vehicle is identified by the captain as an official vehicle and it meets all qualifying requirements.
  • All vehicles must be fully roadworthy and insured to cover the transportation of players to the appropriate venue.

Procedures

  • Team captains should remind drivers of official vehicles that the Expense Reclaim Form is on the Club Spond site in the Club Documents section and advise them of the amount they should be claiming.
  • Repayment rate: 25p per mile for venue mileages set out in the Annex. Any venue not included in the Annex should be calculated by captains using the shortest direct return route on Google Maps from Budehaven School.
  • Submitting claims: Drivers should submit their completed Expenses Reclaim Form to the Treasurer by email as soon as possible after the match.
