Support local hockey

Sponsor a Bude Hockey Club match day

Bude Hockey Club regularly recognises businesses supporting individual match days, home games and match balls. A sponsorship package is a practical way to support local sport while putting your business in front of players, families, visiting teams and the wider Bude community.

Opportunities

Ways to support a fixture

Availability can vary by game, so the club can agree the most suitable package with each sponsor.

Match Day Sponsor

Associate your business with a specific home fixture and receive sponsor recognition around the game.

Home Game Sponsor

Support one of Bude's home fixtures and be included in the club's promotional coverage for that match.

Match Ball Sponsor

A simple way to support a game and receive a visible sponsor thank-you from the club.

Sponsor visibility

What your business can receive

The exact package is agreed with the club. Typical opportunities can include:

Website recognition

Your business name, logo and website link can be displayed on the club website and alongside sponsorship content.

Social media mentions

Pre-match or post-match recognition across Bude Hockey Club's social channels where appropriate.

Match graphics

Opportunity for your logo or business name to appear with a fixture, result or sponsorship graphic.

Banner display

Where venue arrangements allow, the club can discuss displaying an approved company banner at a home match.

Literature & offers

Subject to approval and venue rules, sponsors can discuss displaying leaflets, cards or promotional literature.

Match report credit

Sponsor acknowledgement can be included in club match coverage where that format is used.
