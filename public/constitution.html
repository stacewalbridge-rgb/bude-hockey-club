Bude Hockey Club Constitution

Current club constitution • September 2026

Official club documentThis page presents the wording supplied in the Bude Hockey Club Constitution dated September 2026.

The Name, Location, Objectives and Type of Club

  • The Club shall be named Bude Hockey Club (BHC).
  • The Club will be a member of England Hockey, West Hockey and Cornwall Hockey Association.
  • The Club colours shall be sky blue, with an away strip of black, pink or white as required.
  • The Club shall abide by the rules, policies and jurisdiction of England Hockey.

The objectives of the Club shall be:

  • To promote and provide the opportunity for all people to participate in hockey.
  • The recruitment and development of children, young people and adults into the sport of hockey as players, leaders/coaches, umpires and administrators.
  • The provision of playing, leading/coaching, umpiring and administrative opportunities at a participation, development and performance level.
  • To provide equality of opportunity across all areas of the club, to encourage inclusion by minority groups.
  • To provide a safe, effective and child friendly environment in which to play and learn hockey.

Membership

Membership is open to all individuals. Membership is subject to the Executive Committee approval, the completion of a membership form, parental consent (when under 18) and the full payment of the annual Membership fees.

Membership fees will be determined by the Executive Committee and ratified by the Members at the Club’s Annual General Meeting.

The Club membership year shall run from 1st October to 30th September annually, for all membership categories. This means that all previous season’s Club members are registered and fully paid up to the following 30th September.

All members must pay their Membership fee, either in full, or by agreed instalments, by 30th September in any membership year in order to be covered under the England Hockey Personal Accident Insurance.

Membership categories

  • Adult – aged 18 or over
  • Student – in school Year 12 or above, in full-time further education, registered unemployed, apprentice
  • Junior Playing Adult Hockey – a junior U18, playing senior hockey
  • Junior – U16 not playing senior hockey
  • Occasional – a player who will play no more than 6 league games in the season. If the player plays more than 6 league games they must join as a full member in the category appropriate for their age
  • Associate – non-playing member who acts in a voluntary capacity in support of the Club
  • Honorary – a non-playing member, or former member, may be invited to become an Honorary member at the discretion of the Club Committee

All Members must abide by the Club’s policies and procedures. Only fully paid-up Members are eligible for selection. The Committee has the right to refuse or cancel a membership if the individual has failed to abide by the rules and policies of the Club, and in so doing bringing the club into disrepute.

Committee and Officers

The affairs of BHC shall be managed by the Executive Committee of BHC, except for matters reserved for the club in Annual General Meeting.

The Club will have a President, a role that is not specifically tied to the executive operation of the Club. The President has a vote, and is able to attend Executive Committee meetings if they so wish.

The Executive Committee shall consist of: Chair, Club Secretary, Club Treasurer, Welfare Officer, the Captain of the Ladies teams, the Captain of the Men’s teams, Junior Coordinator, Club Fundraiser.

The Club shall have non-executive functional roles that include, but are not limited to, Membership Secretary, Social Secretary, Umpire Coordinator, Communications Officer, Social Secretary.

BHC Committee may appoint one or more additional officers to BHC Committee in an ex-officio capacity for such purposes as it may deem appropriate. It may also set up sub-committees that would be responsible to the Executive Committee.

The Executive Committee shall meet on a regular basis in order to manage the affairs of the Club effectively and efficiently.

Committee meetings shall be chaired by the Chair, or a nominated Officer of the Club. Meetings shall be organised by the Club Secretary giving 7 days’ notice of the agenda, time and location. A meeting shall not take place until at least 4 members of the Committee, of whom at least one must be either the Chairman, or Club Secretary, are present. Minutes shall be taken and any actions recorded. Any member present at the meeting may act as recorder. Minutes should be disseminated to attendees and other relevant recipients within 7 days of the meeting.

The Committee may be eligible for re-election on an annual basis. No Executive Committee member shall serve a term longer than nine years, without a break from that specific role.

Powers of the Committee

The Committee has the power to raise funds and invite sponsorship to help towards the running costs of the Club, in order to assist in achieving the aims and objectives of the Club (subject to objectives clause above).

Payments or Benefits

The BHC Committee may appoint paid part-time individuals for specific hockey coaching development projects. Such projects are at the complete discretion of the Committee; however, they must be BHC projects. Examples of such appointments are: coaches for development programmes, Leader qualification course tutors etc.

The appointment of any and every paid member of staff must be reviewed and confirmed at each Executive Committee meeting of BHC.

The Club will continue to operate as an amateur community sports club and, as such, will not seek to pay volunteers, whether drawn from the membership or other volunteers, to undertake their role. This includes any arrangement for payment in lieu in the form of a reduction in annual membership fees.

Annual and Extraordinary General Meetings

An Annual General Meeting of Bude Hockey Club shall be held at a date to be set at the first meeting of the Committee held after the final league games of the season every year. The AGM shall be held no later than 30th June in each calendar year.

Notice of the time and date of the Annual General Meeting shall be posted to members, via social media or email, allowing a notice period of at least 14 days before the date of such a meeting, together with the annual accounts and any other relevant annual reports from Committee members. An Agenda for the meeting shall be posted with the notice of the AGM.

The following subjects shall be decided by a vote at the AGM (or a General Meeting):

  • Election of Officers of the Club
  • The setting of membership fees for the coming season, and the approval of the Club budget
  • The approval of the accounts for the immediate past season
  • Any other business accepted by the Chair

All motions to be voted on at any General Meeting must be itemised on the Agenda for that meeting. Club members shall be entitled to put forward motions to be voted on at a General Meeting by informing the Club Secretary in writing, with the proviso that such an item must be notified to the Club Secretary giving sufficient time for the item to be included on the Agenda (14 days’ notice).

Every fully paid-up member of BHC shall be entitled to be present and to vote at any General Meeting of the Club and, should there be equality on any voting issue, the Chairperson of the meeting shall have the casting vote.

At an AGM, 10 members shall form a quorum, provided both the Ladies and Men are each represented by a minimum of 4 persons. In the event of an inquorate meeting the AGM shall be rescheduled to take place within four weeks of the original date. Should a rescheduled AGM also be inquorate, all items reserved for the AGM shall default to the Executive Committee to decide, including the co-option of Executive Committee members other than by election, agreement of membership fees for the forthcoming season.

An Extraordinary General Meeting (EGM) may be called at any time by the BHC Committee or by not less than fifteen members making a written request to BHC Committee for such a meeting. In each case, the items to be discussed shall be placed on an agenda and such agenda, together with the formal notice of such meeting, shall be sent to all club members giving at least 14 days’ notice. The EGM shall not discuss any other business unless directed by the Executive Committee. 15 members shall constitute a quorum for an EGM.

Finance

Bude Hockey Club shall have a bank account in the name of the Club for banking purposes and the choice of the bank shall be at the discretion of the Committee.

The authorised signatories on all club bank accounts shall be any two from the Chair, Treasurer and Secretary of the Club.

The Treasurer, in consultation with BHC Committee, shall be responsible for recording all financial income and expenditure of the Club and for presenting audited/independently verified annual accounts, including a Balance Sheet, and any relevant financial reports to the members at each Annual General Meeting of the Club.

The Treasurer shall present the AGM with a budget for the forthcoming financial year, to be approved by the members at the AGM at the same time that the membership fee proposal is ratified. The financial year of the Club will run from 1st May to 30th April in any calendar year.

Changes to the Constitution of the Club

The Constitution may be altered, added to, or revoked and replaced in accordance with clubs and association law, by majority comprising two-thirds or more of the members present and entitled to vote at any General Meeting of the Club, provided that 14 days’ notice of the proposed amendments has been given to all members.

Conduct of Members

The BHC Committee must adopt and comply with the policies and procedures of England Hockey, and must ensure that all members of the Club follow such policies and procedures.

The BHC Committee may suspend the membership of any member whose conduct has been, in the opinion of the BHC Committee and in line with England Hockey policies, contrary to the interests of the Club.

Disciplinary and Appeals Process

A Disciplinary Committee, consisting of the Secretary, relevant Club Captain and Chairperson shall be responsible for disciplinary hearings of members who infringe the Club’s rules/regulations/constitution, or the rules of hockey. This Committee will be responsible for taking any action of suspension or discipline following such hearings.

The Disciplinary Committee shall meet to hear complaints within 10 days of the advice of a Red Card playing offence, or a complaint being lodged in writing by an opposing team or by a BHC player.

The Disciplinary Committee has the power to take appropriate disciplinary action, including the termination of membership.

The outcome of a disciplinary hearing should be notified in writing to the person who committed the playing offence, or the member who lodged a complaint and the member against whom the complaint was made, within 10 days of the hearing.

There will be a right of appeal to the Disciplinary Committee following disciplinary action being notified. A further appeals panel of three officers not previously involved, drawn from the remainder of the Club’s Committee, should consider the appeal within 10 days of the Secretary receiving the written appeal request.

Liabilities and Indemnities

Members shall not be liable for the debts or liabilities of the Club beyond any unpaid membership fees, match fees or other sums properly due from them to the Club, except to the extent that liability cannot lawfully be excluded.

Officers, Committee members and members of sub-committees shall be indemnified out of the funds of the Club against liabilities, costs and expenses properly incurred in the proper discharge of their duties on behalf of the Club, should such duties not be covered by England Hockey Membership + insurance (which the Club shall subscribe to on an annual renewal basis), except to the extent arising from their own fraud, dishonesty, recklessness, wilful misconduct or other liability that cannot lawfully be indemnified.

Dissolution of the Club

At least 15 Members of the Club may request an Extraordinary General Meeting to be convened at which a resolution to formally dissolve the Club would be tabled. At least 28 days’ notice of such an Extraordinary General Meeting shall have been sent to all members.

If the Club shall pass in an Extraordinary General Meeting, by a majority comprising two-thirds or more of all Members entitled to vote, a resolution of intention to dissolve the Club, the BHC Committee shall take immediate steps to convert all the assets of the Club into money.

The proceeds of the conversion shall be used by a representative BHC Committee, the Dissolution Committee, specifically set up to deal with the dissolution of the Club: firstly, to discharge all the debts and liabilities of the Club; secondly, any balance shall be passed on to another organisation with similar objectives as agreed by the EGM, such as a successor club, in order to continue the development of hockey within the community, targeting youth development in Bude and the surrounding area through school and club coaching.

If no such successor club is present in the Bude area, the proceeds of conversion shall be passed to the Cornwall Hockey Association to form a bursary for junior hockey development in the county.
